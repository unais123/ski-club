Intermediate Snowboarding Course

For riders who can link turns and want more mountain. Six days from dynamic linked turns to carving, switch riding and your first Gondola descents on Apharwat's blue and red terrain.

The Course

From linked turns to carved arcs

This is the bridge between 'I can get down' and 'I can ride'. Over six days we smooth out your linked turns, introduce carving on both edges, add switch riding, and take you onto the variable snow, trees and Gondola terrain where real snowboarding begins.

Coached in small groups by rider-only instructors who ride Apharwat every day, with all-mountain gear and lift access included.

₹36,900 / person · course only · Dec–Mar

Day by Day

Six days from linking to Gondola

Day 01

Refresh & Assess

Warm up on familiar terrain to refresh edge control and falling-leaf, so your coach can tailor the week to exactly where you ride.

Edge controlAssessmentFalling leaf
Day 02

Dynamic Linked Turns

Smooth, continuous heel-to-toe transitions with rhythm and flow — the moment riding starts to feel effortless.

Turn linkingRhythmFlow
Day 03

Carving Foundations

Tip the board on edge and let its sidecut carve clean arcs on gentle groomers, building edge grip and body angulation.

Edge gripAngulationCarving
Day 04

Switch & Variable Snow

Ride switch on easy terrain, then take your turns into crud, soft snow and your first tree lines for all-mountain confidence.

Switch ridingTreesVariable snow
Day 05

The Gondola Opens Up

Ride Phase 1 and lower Phase 2 for long descents, linking the week's skills on bigger blue and red terrain.

GondolaLong descentsRed runs
Day 06

Consolidate & Certify

Mileage on your favourite runs, video feedback and an intermediate progression certificate before departure.

MileageVideo feedbackCertificate

The Mountain

Where this course takes you

You'll progress from the learner meadow onto Gondola Phase 1 and the lower blue and red runs of Phase 2.

Terrain shown is illustrative. Daily run choices depend on snow, weather and avalanche conditions — always follow your instructor and ski patrol on the mountain.
