Bangus Valley

Two vast, linked meadows in Kashmir's far north-west — no hotels, no shops, no noise. Just shepherd camps, trout streams and grass that runs clean to the horizon.

The Valley

Grass to the horizon, silence above

Bangus is two meadows in one — the broad sweep of Boud Bangus and its smaller sibling Lokut Bangus — encircled by dense coniferous ridges in the Kupwara highlands. There is nothing built here: you sleep under canvas and wake to birdsong and mist.

It is the definition of untouched. We keep groups small and our footprint lighter still, carrying everything in and out.

Getting There

How to reach Bangus Valley

Bangus lies in the Kupwara highlands, a scenic drive north from Srinagar followed by a short track into the meadows. There are no hotels here — we camp, so the whole valley is yours.

A local guide and simple registration (arranged by us) are all you need. We carry everything in and out, keeping the meadow exactly as we found it.
