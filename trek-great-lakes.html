Kashmir Great Lakes Trek

Seven days, seven alpine lakes, three high passes — widely called the most beautiful trek in India. Sonamarg to Naranag, across the roof of Kashmir.

7 DaysModerate–ChallengingMax 4,200 mJul – Sep
7 Days / 6 NightsDuration
70+ kmDistance
4,200 mMax Altitude
Jul–SepBest Season

The Trek

A different lake every single day

From the pine meadows of Sonamarg to the sacred twin lakes below Mount Harmukh, the Great Lakes trail strings together the finest alpine scenery in the Kashmir Himalaya. Each pass reveals a new valley; each valley cradles its own impossibly coloured lake.

This is our flagship summer expedition — fully supported with experienced local guides, a camp crew, and pack ponies so you carry only a daypack. All you bring is a reasonable level of fitness and a camera you won't be able to put down.

₹18,500 / person · fixed departures Jul–Sep

Day by Day

The full itinerary

D0

Arrive Srinagar → Sonamarg

Meet at Srinagar, transfer to Sonamarg (2,800 m) through the Sindh valley. Kit check, briefing and a night beside the river to settle in.

TransferBriefing
D1

Sonamarg → Nichnai

A steady climb out of the pines onto open shepherd meadows, camping below Nichnai at around 3,500 m as the treeline falls away behind you.

11 kmMeadows
D2

Nichnai → Vishansar via Nichnai Pass

Cross the Nichnai Pass (4,100 m) and descend to camp beside the deep-blue Vishansar Lake, framed by snow-streaked ridges.

Nichnai PassFirst lake
D3

Vishansar → Gadsar via Gadsar Pass

The trek's high point at 4,200 m, with Vishansar and Kishansar glittering below, then down past the 'lake of flowers' to camp at Gadsar.

4,200 mWildflowers
D4

Gadsar → Satsar

A gentler day through frontier meadows to the seven small lakes of Satsar — braided water, grazing horses and huge skies.

Seven tarnsEasy day
D5

Satsar → Gangbal Twin Lakes

Boulder ridges and a final climb open onto Gangbal and Nundkol beneath the pyramid of Mount Harmukh (5,142 m) — the trek's grand finale.

Mt HarmukhTwin lakes
D6

Gangbal → Naranag → Srinagar

A long, beautiful descent through pine forest to the ancient temple ruins of Naranag, then transfer back to Srinagar.

DescentNaranag temples

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

It is a moderate-to-challenging trek. You cover 70+ km over seven days with three passes above 4,000 m. Comfortable fitness — the ability to walk 12–14 km with a daypack over undulating terrain — is enough with a few weeks of preparation.

The highest point is Gadsar Pass at roughly 4,200 m. Camps are always set lower than the day's high point to aid acclimatisation.

Early July to mid-September, when the passes are clear of snow and the meadows are in full bloom. July offers wildflowers; September offers crisp golden light.

Seven or more, depending on the route and side-trips — Vishansar, Kishansar, Gadsar, Satsar, Gangbal and Nundkol, plus smaller unnamed tarns along the way.

Yes. Groups are led by certified local guides with a support crew, and we host many solo and women travellers every season with dedicated tents on request.
