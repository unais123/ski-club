Snowboarding in Gulmarg

Asia's most underrated freeride mountain, taught by riders who call it home. First turns to first drops — one board, endless winter.

Dec – MarAll LevelsRider-Only CoachesFreeride Terrain

Why Board Here

Powder that forgives, terrain that thrills

Gulmarg's snow is cold, dry and deep — the kind that cushions every learning fall and floats every carved turn. Beginners get wide, mellow gradients; experienced riders get 1,300 vertical metres of lift-served freeride with barely a lift line in sight.

All coaching is by dedicated snowboarders. Your instructor rides what you ride, spots what you feel, and knows exactly which pocket holds the softest snow after a storm.

The Vibe

Small crews, big days

Snowboard weeks run as tight crews — six riders max, one coach, the same faces all week. Expect bonfire debriefs, GoPro film nights and the kind of friendships that only form waist-deep in Himalayan powder.
