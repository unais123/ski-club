The Journey

Six days from beginner to rider

Arrival

Do's, Don'ts & the Concept of Snowboarding

A theory session walking through all the do's and don'ts and the whole concept of basic snowboarding — covering stance, equipment and safe falling before you ever strap in.

Basic stanceSkatingOne-foot glideSafe falling
Day 01

Heel & Toe Edge Control

Learn to engage your heel and toe edges to manage speed and come to a stop — the single most important skill on a snowboard. Everything else builds on it.

Heel-edge stopToe-edge stopBody positionBalance
Day 02

The Falling Leaf Exercise

Slide diagonally across the slope on a single edge, controlling speed by engaging and releasing that edge. Your first real movement across real terrain — lateral, fluid and controlled.

Edge releaseSpeed managementLateral control
Day 03

Heel & Toe Side J-Turns

Your first real turn: ride across the slope and sweep up into a J-shape on both heel and toe edges, learning to steer and initiate direction changes.

Heel-side JToe-side JSteeringTurn initiation
Day 04

Linking Heel & Toe Turns

Combine your heel and toe J-turns into one smooth, continuous transition — the moment snowboarding starts to feel like real riding. Focus on rhythm, flow and reading the slope.

Turn linkingHeel-to-toeRhythm & flow
Day 05

Garlands & Controlled Descents

Partial turns without completing the cross-fall-line transition, building edge awareness and confidence on longer runs — shaping turns with speed and precision.

Partial turnsEdge transitionsBoard control
Day 06

Gondola Phase-I & Side Slip

The ultimate day: riding down from Gondola Phase-I and mastering the side slip. Flatten the board's edges to slide down the fall line, re-engage to stop — and earn your beginner certification.

Gondola Phase 1Side slipCertificationDeparture

Safety First

Your safety is our priority

We hold ourselves to the highest safety standards so you can focus entirely on the mountain, knowing everything else is handled.

1

Trusted & Certified Team

We work only with trusted vendors and certified instructors and guides, all vetted by us.

2

Emergency Medical Cover

In touch with the nearest hospitals and medical facilities at all times for emergencies and evacuations.

3

Doctor on Call 24/7

A local doctor is on call any time of day if necessary — peace of mind for every guest and family.

4

First Aid & CPR Trained

Every crew member is trained in first aid and CPR and prepared for any situation on or off the slopes.

5

Full Safety Gear

Helmets, goggles and protective equipment provided for all activities throughout the program.

6

1:3 Instructor Ratio

A maximum of three students per instructor — every beginner gets personal attention and supervision.

7

Weather & Avalanche Monitoring

Daily weather and avalanche bulletins before every session; activities adjusted or suspended if unsafe.

8

Daily Safety Briefing

Every session begins with a mandatory briefing covering slope rules, fall techniques and emergency procedures.
