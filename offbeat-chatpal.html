Chatpal

A pocket-sized hamlet in Shopian's pine folds that locals call 'mini Pahalgam — before Pahalgam was discovered.' A brook chatters past a handful of homestays; the only traffic is sheep.

The Hamlet

The gentlest escape in Kashmir

Chatpal is barely a dot on the map — a cluster of homes in a fold of pine forest where a clear brook does most of the talking. There is nothing to tick off and everything to slow down for.

It's our favourite first taste of offbeat Kashmir: easy to reach, easy to love, and impossible to rush.
