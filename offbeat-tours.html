The Kashmir beyond the postcards

Four valleys where tourism hasn't yet learned the way: Gurez, Bangus, Chatpal and Daksum. Come while the silence lasts.

May – OctHomestays & CampsNo CrowdsAll Permits Handled

4 Days · From ₹19,900

Gurez Valley

Over the 3,300-metre Razdan Pass lies a valley of log-wood villages threaded along the jade Kishanganga, watched over by the pyramid of Habba Khatoon peak. Gurez is Dard-Shin country — a living culture, a poet-queen's legend, and mountain light that photographers cross continents for.

Bangus Valley

Two vast, linked meadows — Boud Bangus and Lokut Bangus — ringed by coniferous ridges in Kashmir's far northwest. No hotels, no shops, no noise: just shepherd camps, trout streams and grass that runs to the horizon. We camp in deluxe tents and walk wherever curiosity points.

Chatpal

A pocket-sized hamlet in Shopian's pine folds that locals call "mini Pahalgam — before Pahalgam was discovered." A brook chatters past a handful of homestays; trails wander into meadows where the only traffic is sheep. The perfect gentle escape, and our favourite first taste of offbeat Kashmir.

Daksum

Where the Bringhi river rushes cold and loud through dense deodar forest on the old road to Sinthan Top. Daksum is for readers, anglers and anyone whose ideal afternoon is a hammock, a trout rod, or a slow walk to a shepherd's hut for salt tea. Pair it with a Sinthan Top drive for snow even in June.

Frequently asked questions

Indian nationals need no special permits; foreign nationals require simple registrations for Gurez which we arrange in advance. We handle all paperwork either way.

Charming and simple: family-run guesthouses, log-wood homestays and our own deluxe camps. Think hot meals, warm bedding and hosts who treat you like relatives — not room service.

Patchy to none, and honestly that's the point. Postpaid connections work partially in Gurez; Bangus and Chatpal are gloriously offline.

May to October. Gurez and Bangus are at their greenest June–September; Chatpal and Daksum are beautiful from April's blossoms to October's gold.
