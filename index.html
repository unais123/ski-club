The Gulmarg Ski Club Story

Born at 2,650 metres. Raised on powder.

Gulmarg Ski Club began in 2011 with a handful of local instructors and one conviction — that the Meadow of Flowers deserves a ski school worthy of its mountain. Today we guide travellers from across the world down Apharwat's legendary slopes, across turquoise alpine lakes, and into valleys most maps forget.

  • Certified, local-born ski and snowboard instructors
  • Small groups — never more than six learners per coach
  • Full equipment, lift passes and transfers included
  • Luxury stays curated with Gulmarg's finest hotels
