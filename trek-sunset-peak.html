Sunset Peak Expedition

Named for the hour it was made for. A four-day ridge journey from Yusmarg to a west-facing high camp where every evening the Pir Panjal ignites in orange and violet.

The Expedition

Chase the last light of the Pir Panjal

From the quiet meadow resort of Yusmarg, this expedition climbs steadily through fir forest and grazing pastures to a high camp positioned for one thing above all: the sunset. Each clear evening the western ridges glow through every shade of amber and rose.

Summit morning delivers a sweeping panorama, and the whole route stays blissfully free of the crowds found on Kashmir's better-known trails.

₹12,800 / person · fixed departures Jun–Sep
