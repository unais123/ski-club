The Journey

Six days from beginner to skier

Arrival

Do's, Don'ts & the Concept of Skiing

A theory class walking you through the do's and don'ts and the whole concept of basic skiing — touch-based on the techniques to come.

Basic stanceGlidingSide steppingKick turn
Day 01

Snow Plough & Plough Turns

Form a 'pizza slice' or 'V' shape, pushing the tails apart so the inside edges engage — the foundation for controlling speed and stopping. Then initiate plough turns by pressuring one ski's inside edge.

Pizza / V shapeInside edgesWeight transferEdge control
Day 02

Kick Turn

A stationary 180° change of direction: lift one ski tail, swing it around, plant it, then bring the other to match. Essential mobility for cat tracks, steep traverses and tight spaces.

180° turnMobilityTight spaces
Day 03

Traverse Across the Slope

Ski across the slope at an angle rather than straight down. Skis stay parallel as you lean slightly into the hill to engage your uphill edges and stop sliding sideways.

Skis parallelUphill edgesAngle control
Day 04

Stem Turn

The bridge between snow plough and parallel skiing. From a traverse, briefly stem the uphill ski into a small wedge, transfer your weight onto it to complete the turn, and finish with skis parallel.

Stem to parallelWeight transferMomentum
Day 05

Uphill Christie / Uphill Swing

A finishing manoeuvre: after completing a turn, deliberately let your skis skid sideways so they point more uphill, using the rising slope to slow and stop. Builds controlled skidding and rotary skills.

Controlled skiddingRotary skillsStopping
Day 06

First Phase Gondola & Side Slip

The ultimate day: skiing from the first phase of the Gondola and mastering the side slip. Flatten the edges to slide down the fall line, re-engage to stop — an amazing ski experience, and your beginner certification.

Gondola Phase 1Side slipCertificationDeparture

We hold ourselves to the highest safety standards so you can focus entirely on the mountain, knowing everything else is handled.

2
