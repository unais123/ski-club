Daksum

Where the Bringhi river runs cold and loud through dense deodar forest on the old road to Sinthan Top. For readers, anglers, and anyone whose ideal afternoon is a hammock and salt tea.

2 DaysEasyForest RetreatMay – Oct

May–OctSeason

The Retreat

A river, a forest, and nowhere to be

Daksum is deodar forest wrapped around a rushing river, on the quiet old highway toward the Sinthan Top pass. It asks nothing of you but to slow down — cast a line, walk the woods, or simply listen to the Bringhi.

Pair it with a drive to Sinthan Top and you can touch snow in June, then be back beside the river for salt tea by afternoon.

From ₹10,500 / person
